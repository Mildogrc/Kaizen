Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Kaizen (studykaizen.com) is a personal study app run by an individual, not a company. This page says what it stores, why, and who else sees it. The short version: your data is yours, only you can see it, and nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or tracked across sites.

What Kaizen stores

  • Your Google account basics: name, email address, and profile picture, from “Continue with Google”. Kaizen asks Google for nothing else.
  • What you put in: courses, tracks, goals, books and notes, words, review history, settings, uploaded cover images, and AI conversations.
  • Anki data you sync: card and review snapshots from your own Anki collection, read through your browser tab only when you sync.
  • AI keys you add (optional): stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and used only for your own requests.
  • School email (optional): if you verify an @augusta.edu address for free AI, Kaizen stores that address and a monthly count of your free AI usage, to check eligibility and keep the program within budget.
  • Sign-in session: one cookie that keeps you signed in, plus the time zone you choose. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Who can see it

Every account is separate. Other users cannot see your data. The person running Kaizen can technically access the database to keep the service running and fix problems, and does not look at your content otherwise.

Services Kaizen relies on

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and sends verification emails.
  • Neon hosts the database.
  • Google handles sign-in.
  • AI providers: when you press “Ask AI”, your message and the related study context go to the provider you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google). Free AI for verified students runs on Anthropic. Their own privacy terms apply to that processing; API use is not used to train their models under their standard terms.

Your choices

  • Remove an AI key or your school email at any time under Settings → AI.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, email support@studykaizen.com from your account’s email address. Deletion is permanent.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the app will mention it.
